# Career OS Constitution

## Core Principles

### I. Useful attention
Serve real obligations, human judgment, preparation and selective memory. Throughput and visibility are not success proxies. Preserve target habitat and distinguish local usefulness from externally calibrated career options.

### II. Three authoritative files
Context, Active Work and Outcome Log hold state. WorkObject is an attention case. Chat recall, derived briefs and methodology artifacts are not additional state authorities.

### III. Human responsibility
Decide, Prepare and Close are independently usable. Human approval is required for durable operational edits; no autonomous execution or communication. Review responsibility cannot be traded for career value.

### IV. Evidence and uncertainty
Separate observations, assessments and claim limits. Keep unresolved commitments explicit. Missing facts stay unknown. Date changing facts and external references.

### V. Bounded v0
Manual loading, one approved work chat, no infrastructure additions. Scout and Multiplier are situational questions. No mandatory lifecycle, Person store or separate decision collection.
