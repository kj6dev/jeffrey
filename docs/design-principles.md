# Design principles

1. **Adrien is the scarce principal.**
   Optimize where his judgment and deep-focus capacity are spent.

2. **Attention allocation over task throughput.**
   The system exists to help choose the right engagement, not maximize completed tasks.

3. **Substance before visibility.**
   Make excellent consequential work legible; do not optimize performative participation.

4. **Know more than you surface.**
   A useful CoS may hold context silently. It should not notify or question Adrien merely because it can.

5. **Ask only questions that can change the recommendation.**
   Avoid turning every interaction into a questionnaire.

6. **Preparation gets a stopping rule.**
   Prevent both underpreparation and elegant overinvestment.

7. **Commitments are lossless; outcomes are selective.**
   Open promises must survive. Most completed work should leave little durable memory.

8. **Observed fact != assessment.**
   Preserve source-backed observations separately from interpretation and claim limits.

9. **Mobility != local effectiveness.**
   Internal accomplishment is not proof of employability, access, or target-habitat quality.

10. **Systemize from evidence, not imagination.**
    A recurring annoyance is not automatically a reusable system opportunity.

11. **Scout without technology tourism.**
    Check for materially better/newly possible approaches only when they could change the work.

12. **The chat is an interface, not the database.**
    Durable files are authoritative.

13. **Human-approved durable changes.**
    No claim of persistence without confirmed application.

14. **No agent cosplay.**
    Add workers only for bounded work that benefits from separation, not to imitate an org chart.

15. **Expand from observed v0 friction.**
    Brainstormed capabilities go to the parking lot, not the implementation queue.
