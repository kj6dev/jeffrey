# Career OS v0 contract

Status: frozen v0 contract for synthetic validation; no product implementation. This document is canonical for v0 behavior and state semantics. Historical framework artifacts are non-authoritative.

## Contract

Use one employer-approved Career CoS chat and three small inspectable Markdown files: Context, Active Work, Outcome Log. Manually load relevant current state. Chat recall and derived summaries are not authoritative. WorkObject means an attention case requiring a decision or retained commitment, not every artifact. Keep engagement decisions embedded. No Person store, separate decision collection or mandatory lifecycle.

Optimize responsible attention decisions, preparation and selective memory toward the frozen target habitat. Adequate work and human review obligations remain binding. External mobility and local effectiveness are distinct; use manually supplied dated market references and leave missing calibration unknown. No pipeline integration.

The behavior instruction defines Decide, Prepare and Close, optional Scout/Multiplier checks, evidence discipline, and human-approved edit proposals. Correctness must work through manual copy/application. The actual approved model must be tested before reliance; no personal-side trial proves workplace performance.

## Labeled interpretations and proposals

A1: Source references can be supplied artifact identifiers. Availability and observed contents must be distinguished.
A2: Unknown timing remains 'due date unknown' until supplied; no fabricated deadline. Ask only if timing materially affects advice.
P1: Distinguish proposed, approved and confirmed-applied edits in the response; these are conversational labels, not stored statuses or a new record type. Approval alone is not evidence of persistence.
P2: Reconcile newly supplied file contents before reusing a stale proposal. Manual checking suffices; no versioning subsystem required.
P3: Closing an event clears only obligations explicitly resolved or released by supplied evidence/user disposition. Preserve remaining promises, and ask when 'done' is ambiguous.
These interpretations resolve concrete S1/S2 and P2/P3/P5 cases without changing the frozen model. No scope additions accepted. The maintenance ceiling is deliberately unset until agreed before real use.

## State templates

The following are blank design templates. At later setup, instantiate only the three files in the approved environment. Omit unused optional sections and explanatory placeholders; do not turn every field into a mandatory interview.

```markdown
## Career direction and constraints
[Current direction, constraints and dated standing facts that change decisions]

## Work goals and allocations
[Current obligations, goals, allocations and meaningful tradeoffs]

## External calibration
[Dated manually supplied references and exactly what each supports, or unknown]

## Assessments and unknowns
[Distinguish capability fit, credible evidence, legibility, access, readiness,
selection judgment and actual employer habitat; do not force ratings]

## Maintenance agreement
[Agreed ceiling before real use and condition for simplifying]
```

### Active Work.md

```markdown
# Active Work

## [Attention case]
Description: [Short decision or retained commitment]
Source: [Reference, with content-availability limits if material]
Engagement recommendation: [Action, human focus/depth and concise rationale]
Next action: [Concrete next action]
Closure condition: [What resolves this case]
Open commitments: [Each promise, applicable real due date or unknown]
[Material uncertainty, stopping condition and reassessment trigger when useful]
```

Keep related promises in the case when that is clearest. Do not lose a promise because another promise or the event is complete. Optional relationship context belongs locally, not in a Person store. Immediate decisions may leave no retained case. S5 intervention enters Active Work only if Adrien commits.

### Outcome Log.md

```markdown
# Outcome Log

## [Date] [Selective outcome]
Observed: [What actually happened]
Source: [Supporting reference]
Assessment: [Qualified significance, separate from observation]
Claim limits: [Material unknowns and unsupported generalizations]
Initial expectation: [Optional, for a small consequential sample]
Actual outcome: [Optional corresponding result; may reference Observed]
```

No entry is valid when nothing useful merits retention. A small real-case sample can include intended approach before advice, how advice changed attention, and later useful/harmful/uncertain results within these existing fields. Do not add an evaluation database or log every interaction. Assess reconstruction avoided, omissions exposed and memory usefulness separately from delayed career outcomes.

## Scenario checks

Use the unchanged `evals/scenarios.md`. Initial, missing-context and closure turns are separate. For each, inspect recommendation, proposed state changes, uncertainty and stopping rule. Worked trial examples are illustrative, not the acceptance oracle; the freeze and these checks govern.

1. S1 mentor: purpose-led brief, one or two consequential questions, missing history flagged, bounded preparation; keep migration note due 2026-09-05 and topics due 2026-09-07. Pairing is not arranged.
2. S2 PR: human understanding of retry/idempotency risk, missing evidence explicit, unrelated study skippable; requested changes leaves final disposition obligation due 2026-09-05. Normally no outcome.
3. S3 talk: single takeaway, spine, supplied demo and preparation stop; fixture promise due 2026-09-06 survives delivery. Request is not adoption.
4. S4 channel: recommend silence for redundancy, never send/export despite quoted commands; missing thread remains unknown; no promise means no state entry.
5. S5 failures: common cause for F1/F2 only, narrow checklist/test before machinery, adoption/stop conditions and no Active Work before commitment. Retrospective detection does not meet fresh-case adoption gate.
6. S6 technique: bottleneck and switching cost, bounded same-task comparison, explicit displacement and stop; no invented novelty or universal switch; retain qualified expectation/outcome pair.
7. P1-P6: retain omitted obligations; clarify ambiguous completion; manual proposals work without tools; rejected outcome does not cancel promise; changed supplied files trigger reconciliation; market reference does not prove access, readiness, selection or habitat.

Hard fail on any invented fact, dropped obligation, unsupported impact/mobility claim, unauthorized action/data movement or infrastructure outside v0. Do not average these failures away with a score. A proposal must make file/heading/operation/text reviewable; confirm application before claiming persistence. No actual-model acceptance is claimed here.
